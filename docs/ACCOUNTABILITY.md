# TrendForge accountability sheet

Scenario: product launch. Audience: beginner creators, marketers and small online sellers. The creator supplies facts; three explicit AI roles draft and review multiple formats. The creator retains the final decision. This sheet describes implemented limits, not legal certification.

| Boundary | What the system does |
|---|---|
| Sources | Product claims use only the current immutable product-sheet facts and exact evidence quotes. Approved examples guide tone. Selected YouTube metadata supplies creative inspiration only. Owned/licensed uploaded clips or optional stock supply footage and retain provenance; neither establishes new product facts. |
| Inputs | Treats source instructions, testimonials, prior copy, preferences and model output as untrusted data. A testimonial is not proof of efficacy. Schema validation, source-evidence checks and an independent Reviewer supplement the prompts. |
| Rules | Versioned snapshots cover facts, cure/treatment claims, advertising, verified halal evidence, brand tone, multilingual consistency and instruction boundaries. Rule IDs and handbook basis appear beside rejections. These are documented workspace rules, not invented BPOM statute citations. |
| Permissions | Cookie sessions require CSRF protection. Personal, hashed, revocable connector tokens access only their owner's projects. Models receive no database credentials, session cookies, shell access, publication tools or provider keys. |
| Actions | Planner chooses an angle and facts, Writer produces formats/languages, and Reviewer checks every line. After content approval, the creator selects a script, uploads 3–5 clips and approves a trim/sequence. FFmpeg renders a vertical draft, faster-whisper transcribes actual Indonesian audio, and the creator corrects and approves subtitles before final MP4 export. |
| Limits | At most three Writer/Reviewer rounds and a configurable execution budget (default 3 minutes). Explicit medicine requests can halt at Planner for clarification. Malformed results, missing line checks, quota failures, interrupted runs and unresolved findings escalate; they never approve content. |
| Approval | Only a signed-in website operator can approve the exact current, reviewed revision. A changed source or edited draft invalidates its release eligibility. The connector cannot approve or export. Every proposed tone rule needs separate human approval and cannot override mandatory rules. |
| Publication | Text ZIP requires reviewed content and human approval. Video production requires a binding to that exact approved script and separate cut/subtitle approvals. Final files are vertical H.264/AAC MP4 and optional SRT. Source changes invalidate linked video release. No social account is accessed and nothing is automatically published. |
| Record | Project-owned source snapshots, role exchanges, rejected copy, fixes, parent revisions, approval actor/time and export events persist in Turso or local SQLite. Only that owner can read them. Draft/audit records currently remain until the owner removes the project; there is no automatic audit-retention expiry or separate tamper-proof archive. |
| External processing | WorkBuddy receives supplied product facts, selected outputs, rules and the owner's approved examples through the connector, and processes them under the creator's own WorkBuddy account and its terms. Use fictional/public facts for the demo. Connector tokens and other credentials remain in the backend or the creator's WorkBuddy configuration; none appear in public status or content exports. |
| Limitations | Semantic review and speech transcription can make mistakes. Creators must watch the video, check spoken claims and correct subtitles. Deterministic checks do not prove legal compliance. Certificate verification is a creator attestation, not a registry check. Cover verification checks SVG text, not OCR. The example video uses clearly labeled owned synthetic test footage. |

Evidence: synthetic automated cases and real Gemini generation/rejection/repair records are listed in [CHALLENGE_C.md](CHALLENGE_C.md). Actual WorkBuddy execution, official organizer cases, public repository publication and a recorded demo must be evidenced separately; they are not implied by installing a connector.
